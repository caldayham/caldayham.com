Field360: Client Recordings to Proposals

Project Type: Software / Construction Tooling

Timeline: 2026

Technologies: Next.js, Supabase, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Claude

Demo

Overview

Field360 turns a recorded client consultation into a complete proposal. I record the conversation and take photos on site. The app transcribes the audio and drafts a bid broken into line items, with timeline estimates based on past projects in the system.

How It Works

  • Pair consultation audio with site photos
  • Transcribe the recording with Gemini 2.5 Flash
  • Generate a line-item bid and timeline from past project data
  • Edit the proposal: custom paragraphs, past review snippets, images
  • Send it for signature, then send invoices the client pays from their bank account in the client portal
